# CREATE A PREMIUM FRIENDS-INSPIRED PERSONAL PORTFOLIO WEBSITE FOR A FINAL-YEAR COMPUTER SCIENCE STUDENT

Create a complete, production-quality, visually exceptional personal portfolio website for a **final-year Computer Science and Engineering student who is currently working as a Software Developer Intern**.

The website must combine two identities into one cohesive experience:

1. A technically strong, professional software developer portfolio suitable for recruiters, hiring managers, software engineers, and internship/full-time opportunities.
2. A cute, aesthetic, warm, nostalgic, playful personal visual identity inspired by the overall visual feeling of the **FRIENDS television show**, especially its iconic purple picture-frame aesthetic.

The final website should feel like a **personal digital scrapbook belonging to a Computer Science student**, rather than a conventional developer portfolio.

The desired design direction is:

**FRIENDS-inspired × Cute Female Developer × Butter Yellow × Purple Frames × Scrapbook × Polaroids × Soft 3D × Interactive Portfolio**

The website must be memorable, polished, responsive, technically credible, and highly personalized.

---

## 1. RESUME MUST BE THE SOURCE OF TRUTH

The attached resume contains the actual professional information of the portfolio owner.

Before creating the website, carefully inspect and understand the complete resume.

Extract all relevant information from it, including:

* Full name
* Professional title
* Career summary
* About information
* Current internship
* Previous internships
* Work experience
* Companies
* Job titles
* Employment dates
* Education
* Degree
* University/college
* Graduation year
* CGPA or percentage
* Programming languages
* Frameworks
* Libraries
* Databases
* Development tools
* Cloud technologies
* AI/ML technologies
* Data science technologies
* Relevant coursework
* Projects
* Project descriptions
* Technologies used
* Key contributions
* Achievements
* Awards
* Hackathons
* Certifications
* Positions of responsibility
* Leadership experience
* GitHub
* LinkedIn
* Email
* Other professional links

The resume is the **single source of truth for factual content**.

Do not fabricate any information.

Do not invent technologies because they are common for Computer Science students.

Do not invent projects.

Do not invent achievements.

Do not invent job responsibilities.

Do not invent metrics.

Do not invent companies.

Do not invent certifications.

Do not invent social media links.

Do not invent testimonials.

Do not invent client logos.

Do not create fake statistics such as "20+ projects" or "95% Python proficiency" unless the resume explicitly supports those claims.

You may rewrite resume content into cleaner, shorter, more engaging portfolio language, but the meaning and facts must remain accurate.

The goal is to transform the resume into a compelling visual story, not to copy-paste the resume into HTML.

# 2. OVERALL CREATIVE DIRECTION

The website should immediately feel different from a typical software developer portfolio.

Avoid the common visual language of:

* Black backgrounds
* Neon gradients
* Blue corporate dashboards
* Generic glassmorphism
* Generic coding illustrations
* Standard rectangular cards
* Excessive glowing effects
* Template-like layouts

Instead, create a warm and charming environment.

Imagine entering a **beautifully designed digital bedroom/workspace/scrapbook of a Computer Science student**, where technical achievements are displayed through photographs, notes, frames, stickers, illustrations, and small animated objects.

The design must remain sophisticated.

The website should communicate:

> "She is a serious software developer, but she also has a creative personality."

The visual personality should never compromise professional credibility.

---

# 3. PRIMARY COLOR PALETTE

The dominant background across the entire website must be a **light buttery yellow**.

The butter-yellow color should become one of the strongest recognizable characteristics of the portfolio.

Use a soft, creamy, warm palette rather than a saturated yellow.

Suggested colors include:

* Butter yellow: #FFF4C2
* Soft cream: #FFF8DE
* Warm ivory: #FFFDF3
* Light beige: #F8EFCF
* Dark brown: #2B2118
* Soft brown: #604C3A
* FRIENDS-inspired purple: #6E4A8D
* Muted purple: #84639B

Do not use too many additional colors.

The overall visual formula should remain approximately:

**70% butter yellow/cream**

**20% dark brown/black**

**10% purple and subtle accent colors**

Purple should be particularly prominent in the hero frame and selected interactive elements.

Dark brown should be used instead of pure black wherever possible because it will harmonize better with the warm background.
